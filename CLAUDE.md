# Mu-Zueki

A weekly finance-news hip hop reel, made after Friday's close for the trading week just ended (an ISO week such as `2026-W44`): `pipeline/` (Python) gathers the news and writes and generates the song, `reel/` (HyperFrames) is the video. `README.md` describes the layout and the week-to-week use.

- When asked to run the pipeline, make a reel or redo a week's video, follow `.claude/skills/run-pipeline/SKILL.md`. The video step always goes in this order: ask the user for a reference video (their own, a default template, or one you search for); brainstorm the video by going through every resource in the skill's table (stock footage, illustrations, interface elements, reference films, the HyperFrames registry, past reels, pixelfx, Gemini frames); show the user the idea and a line-by-line picture plan and get a yes; only then build.
- A reel is pictures with the lyric over them, not the lyric alone: full 9:16, clean corners, every shot layered from several resources under one grade so it looks studio-made. The animation is pixelfx effects (`reel/lib/pfx.js`, `reel/lib/pixelfx/`) with motion graphics. The skill lists the rules the user has set.
- The audio stage spends ElevenLabs credits, and so do `pipeline.revoice` and `pipeline.ambience` (the stem split). Say so before running them. Gemini image generation has a daily quota: say how many frames a job needs before starting.
- Sound effects (`pipeline/sound.py`, skill section 3g): the picture records its cues, real-sfx (`.claude/skills/real-sfx`) places real recordings from the Openverse library (`pipeline/openverse.py`, CC0 / CC BY only) on them over the song, and real-sfx and earshot (`D:/Projects/Experiments/earshot`, its own Python) critique the result. Never duck or change the song. Report their numbers, never how something sounds.
- If Claude in Chrome is not connected, start Chrome yourself (`Start-Process "C:\ProgramData\Microsoft\Windows\Start Menu\Programs\Google Chrome.lnk"`) and retry before asking the user.
- Ambience (`AMBIENCE.md`, `pipeline/ambience.py`) is added to a song before its video is started, and kept subtle: it must never affect the song or make the voice sound artificial. The video is set in the same place.
- Tests: `.venv\Scripts\python.exe -m unittest discover tests`.
- `output/`, `reel/audio.mp3`, `reel/data/`, `reel/media/`, `reel/renders/` and `reel/snapshots/` are generated.
- `instagram/` is the account folder: `posts/WEEK/` holds each reel, its description and `post.json` (draft or posted). Keep it in step with every reel you render. Never post without the user approving the reel and then the description.
